How It Works

From complaint to resolution in four smart steps

1
Submit

Enter your name, USN/Faculty ID, device type, location and describe the issue.

2
Classify

DFA + Regex validates the location. NLP keyword matching classifies the fault category.

3
Route

The routing table maps your zone to the right admin. Backups and queuing handle overload.

4
Resolve

Track your complaint in real time. The admin updates status at each step until resolved.
